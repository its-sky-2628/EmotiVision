HOW IT WORKS

From camera to emotion insight.

EmotiVision turns a camera frame into an emotion prediction through a simple AI processing pipeline.

1

Camera or Image

The user starts the webcam or uploads an image containing a face.

2

Frame Capture

The browser captures a camera frame and sends it securely to the Flask backend.

3

Face Detection

The backend receives the image and uses computer vision to locate and analyze the face.

4

Emotion Model

DeepFace analyzes facial expressions and returns emotion probabilities.

5

Result

The interface displays the dominant emotion and confidence score.
