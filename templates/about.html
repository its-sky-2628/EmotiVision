ABOUT EMOTIVISION

Built to make emotion AI simple.

What is EmotiVision?

EmotiVision is an AI-powered facial expression detection project designed to identify visible emotional expressions from a camera feed or uploaded image.

It combines a modern web interface, Flask backend, computer vision and DeepFace-based emotion analysis.

👨‍💻

Created By

Shreyansh Yadav

Project creator and developer of the EmotiVision AI emotion detection application.

🎯

Why it was created

The project was created to demonstrate how AI and computer vision can interpret visible facial expressions in an easy-to-use web application.

🚀

Benefits

It can help with AI demonstrations, academic projects, computer-vision learning, prototyping and emotion-aware interface experiments.

Important Note

Emotion prediction is an AI estimate based on visible facial patterns. It should not be treated as a medical or psychological diagnosis, and results can vary with lighting, camera quality and facial pose.
